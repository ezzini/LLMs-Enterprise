## Systematic Literature Review Methodology (Transferred from Paper)

### Inclusion Criteria:
- Studies showing implementation details of chatbots using RAG technology.
- Studies illustrating RAG technology performance and impact.
- Comparative studies between RAG chatbots and any standalone LLMs, toolkits, or cloud-based chatbots.
- Studies illustrating models used to enhance RAG (e.g., Mistral and Llama).
- Empirical experiments evaluating overall chatbot performance, security, retrieval quality, or other metrics.
- Papers published in 2020-2025.
- Published articles (journals, conferences, or theses, not preprints).

### Exclusion Criteria:
- Papers published before 2020.
- Non-English papers.
- Papers discussing LLM or AI chatbots without implementing RAG.
- Grey literature (blogs, community posts, opinion pieces).
- Non-empirical papers.
- Duplicate studies.

### Quality Evaluation Criteria:
1. Is the study empirical?
2. Does the study provide sufficient details about methodology and experiments?
3. Does the author demonstrate how the model was tested?
4. Does the paper include a comparative model?
5. Does the data source exist?
6. Is security mentioned?
7. Does the author state the limitations explicitly?
